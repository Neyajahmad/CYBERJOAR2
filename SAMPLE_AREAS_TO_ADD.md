## 📊 Growth Score Formula

```
Growth Score = (0.4 × Price Growth) + (0.3 × Rental Demand) + (0.3 × Infrastructure)
```

### Score Ranges:
- **85-100**: 🔥 Very High Growth (Red on heatmap)
- **70-84**: 📈 High Growth (Orange/Yellow)
- **50-69**: 📊 Medium Growth (Yellow/Green)
- **0-49**: 🌱 Emerging Growth (Green)

---

## 💡 Tips for Adding Areas:

1. **Price Growth**: How much property prices increased (0-100)
2. **Rental Demand**: How high is rental demand (0-100)
3. **Infrastructure**: Quality of roads, metro, schools, hospitals (0-100)

### High Growth Areas (85+):
- IT hubs (Gurgaon, Whitefield, Hitech City)
- Premium locations (Bandra, Koramangala)
- Metro connectivity areas

### Medium Growth Areas (70-84):
- Developing suburbs
- Areas with upcoming metro
- Residential zones

### Emerging Areas (50-69):
- Tier-2 cities
- Outskirts of metros
- New development zones
